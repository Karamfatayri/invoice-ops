Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. When you upgrade, the new rate applies immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

Absolutely. We are SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and ISO 27001 certified. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Data residency options available for EU customers.
